**43. Internal Bar Strength (IBS) mean reversion** [SR]
- Intuition: Where a bar closes within its range reveals intraday buying/selling exhaustion; extreme closes revert next period.
- Formula: IBS = (Close − Low)/(High − Low) ∈ [0,1]. **Example rule:** buy when IBS < 0.2; exit when IBS ≥ 0.5. **Caveat:** the entry requires the bar's close — execution is on the next bar.
- Data: Daily or minute OHLC bars.
- Sources: Any OHLCV vendor.
- Horizon: 1 day (daily); sub-session on minute bars.
