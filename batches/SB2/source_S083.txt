**83. Imbalance/tick/volume/dollar bars** [D]
- Intuition: Clock bars mix busy and empty minutes; sampling in event/volume/dollar time makes bars informationally uniform; imbalance bars close when signed flow hits a threshold, concentrating information arrival.
- Formula: Tick bars: every N trades; volume bars: every V shares; dollar bars: every $D traded. Imbalance bars: track θ_T = Σ b_t (b_t = ±1 tick sign); close the bar when |θ_T| ≥ E[θ_T] threshold (rolling expected imbalance).
- Data: Tick trades.
- Sources: Any trade feed.
- Horizon: endogenous bar length (seconds at the open, minutes at lunch); a feature-engineering step.
- Citations: Easley, López de Prado & O'Hara volume clocks; López de Prado, *Advances in Financial ML* (2018), ch. 2.
- Coverage: G.
