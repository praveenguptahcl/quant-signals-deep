**85. Triple-barrier labeling** [D]
- Intuition: Fixed-horizon "did price go up?" labels ignore path and stops; label by which barrier is hit first.
- Formula: For event time t: upper = P_t·(1+σ·k), lower = P_t·(1−σ·k), vertical barrier t+H (σ = vol estimate, e.g., ATR or daily vol); scan the path; first touch wins (+1/−1/0); optionally volatility-scale barriers and drop 0 labels or keep a hold class.
- Data: Price path at execution granularity (tick or 1-min).
- Sources: Any intraday feed.
- Horizon: H = the chosen vertical barrier; a labeling procedure, not a signal.
- Citations: López de Prado, *AFML* (2018), ch. 3.
- Coverage: G.
