**88. Purged / embargoed cross-validation** [D]
- Intuition: In overlapping financial labels, standard K-fold leaks; purge training samples whose label interval overlaps the test set, and embargo after each test fold.
- Formula: Purge: drop train samples with [t0,t1] ∩ test interval ≠ ∅; embargo: drop train samples within H (label horizon) after each test fold's end; then K-fold on the remainder.
- Data: Any labeled dataset with overlapping horizons.
- Sources: Any (validation layer).
- Horizon: a validation procedure, not a signal.
- Citations: López de Prado, *AFML* (2018), ch. 7.
- Coverage: G.
