**34. Scheduled macro-announcement drift** [D]
- Intuition: Volatility and directional drift concentrate around scheduled releases (FOMC, CPI, payrolls); pre-announcement drift and post-announcement momentum are tradable.
- Formula: Event calendar; measure pre-event drift (e.g., 30 min before) and trade continuation for 15–60 min after the print.
- Data: 1-min bars + economic calendar.
- Sources: Any intraday vendor + calendar feed.
- Horizon: minutes to 1 hour around the event.
- Citations: Ederington & Lee (1993) document the volatility-spike pattern.
- Coverage: R1, T.
