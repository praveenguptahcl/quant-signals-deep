**38. Sub-hour microstructure reversal** [D]
- Intuition: At horizons under an hour, returns negatively autocorrelate due to bid-ask bounce and temporary liquidity imbalances — fade sharp 5–30 minute moves.
- Formula: r_{t:t+h} = α − β·r_{t−h:t} + ε with β > 0 for h < 60 min; or simply signal = −r over the last 5–15 minutes, entered with limit orders earning the spread.
- Data: Minute bars or ticks.
- Sources: Any intraday feed.
- Horizon: minutes to < 1 hour.
- Citations: Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka (2010) ("short-term reversal driven by temporary liquidity imbalances lasting less than an hour and bid-ask bounce"); Jegadeesh (1990)/Lehmann (1990) for the daily/weekly analog.
- Coverage: R1.
