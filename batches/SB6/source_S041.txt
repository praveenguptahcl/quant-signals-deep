**41. Bollinger-band reversal** [D]
- Intuition: A close beyond kσ of a moving mid is a ~2-sigma excursion; fade back to the mid when the impulse fails to expand. (The breakout use of the same bands is family B.)
- Formula: Mid = SMA(n), upper/lower = SMA ± k·σ (n=20, k=2 conventional); %b = (P − lower)/(upper − lower); bandwidth = (upper − lower)/mid. Fade: touch the band then close back inside; target = mid.
- Data: OHLC, 1–15 min or daily.
- Sources: Any OHLCV vendor.
- Horizon: minutes to 1–2 days.
- Citations: Bollinger, *Bollinger on Bollinger Bands*.
- Coverage: G, T, C.
