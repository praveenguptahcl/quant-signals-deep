## Q-SB8-1: Formulas + worked examples — VERBATIM (key content)

### 1. Bipower variation
r_{t,i} = log return over intraday interval i, i=1..n. RV_t = Σ r_{t,i}². BV_t = (1/μ₁²) Σ_{i=2}^{n} |r_{t,i-1}||r_{t,i}|; μ₁ = E|Z| = √(2/π); 1/μ₁² = π/2. **BV_t = (π/2) Σ_{i=2}^{n} |r_{t,i-1}||r_{t,i}|.** RV_t − BV_t ≈ jump contribution to quadratic variation. Bipower robust to isolated jumps: adjacent-return products less affected than squared returns. [citation: ssrn.com]
Practical: 5-min sampling common starting point (1-min/5-min/15-min alternatives; 5-min limits microstructure noise for liquid assets). Annualization: RV_ann = RV_daily × 252 (illustrative). Jump proxy J_t = max(RV_t − BV_t, 0) — truncation practical, not part of definition.

### 2. Intraday GARCH(1,1)
r_{t,i} = μ_{t,i} + ε_{t,i}; ε_{t,i} = σ_{t,i} z_{t,i}. **σ_{t,i}² = ω + α ε_{t,i-1}² + β σ_{t,i-1}²**; ω>0, α≥0, β≥0; covariance-stationary needs **α+β<1**; unconditional Var(r) = ω/(1−α−β). Time-of-day pattern: separate deterministic periodic volatility: r_{t,i} = s_i ε_{t,i}, s_i = intraday seasonal scale factor (avg vol for that 5-min interval).
Practical: 5-min returns; estimation window 20–60 days (~1,500–5,000 five-min obs); shorter 10–20d for fast markets; 60–120d for stability; persistence α+β ≈ 0.90–0.99 (illustrative); heavy tails → Student-t innovations.

### 3. Variance risk premium
**VRP_t = IV_t^{ann} − E_t[RV_{t,T}^{ann}]**; ex-post VRP_t^{ex-post} = IV_t^{ann} − RV_{t,T}^{ann}. Volatility-premium analog: VRP_t^σ = IVol_t − √(RV_{t,T}^{ann}) [page render ambiguous on sqrt — confirm from canonical source before coding].
Model-free implied variance (discrete): IV_t^{ann} = (2e^{rT}/T)[ Σ_{K_i<K_0} (ΔK_i/K_i²) P(K_i) + Σ_{K_i>K_0} (ΔK_i/K_i²) C(K_i) ] − (1/T)(F_t/K_0 − 1)²; T in years; r cont. risk-free; F_t forward; K_0 first strike ≤ F_t; ΔK_i strike spacing. [Federal Reserve Board]
Practical: 30-calendar-day horizon; realized = 5-min RV, bipower, or forecast; **interpolate TOTAL variance w(K,T) = σ_IV²(K,T)·T, never raw vol, across maturities**; across strikes linear in log-moneyness k=ln(K/F).

### 4. 25-delta risk reversal
**RR_25 = σ_IV^{call,25Δ} − σ_IV^{put,25Δ}**. Positive = upside calls richer; negative = downside puts richer. Δ_C(K_C)=+0.25, Δ_P(K_P)=−0.25. BS w/ div yield q: Δ_C = e^{−qT}N(d₁), Δ_P = e^{−qT}[N(d₁)−1]; d₁ = [ln(S/K)+(r−q+σ²/2)T]/(σ√T). Black-76 forward-delta convention for futures options — keep consistent. Practical: 10Δ/15Δ also used; delta tolerance ±0.5–±1.0 points [render partially garbled — confirm]; same expiry both legs; document spot vs forward vs premium-adjusted delta convention; numerical solver for strike at closest delta.

### 5. Gamma exposure per strike
OI_net(K) = OI_C(K) − OI_P(K) (calls positive, puts negative — market convention, NOT accounting identity; some datasets flip put sign; some analysts model dealer positioning from customer trades instead). BS gamma: Γ(K) = e^{−qT}φ(d₁)/(Sσ√T). **GEX(K) = Γ(K)·OI_net(K)·M·S²·(0.01)** = Γ(K)[OI_C−OI_P]MS²/100 — dollars of P&L for 1% underlying move. GEX_total = Σ_K GEX(K). M=100 equity options.
Practical: strikes within 10–20% of spot; expiries nearest weekly → 6–12 months; OI usually lagged vs trades; use each option's own IV and T for Γ; report per-strike + total; state per-1%-move vs per-1-point vs unscaled.

## Q-SB8-3: Documented evidence — VERBATIM (key content; no worked trading P&L)

**Lead: documented conditional value, but published effects much weaker than raw backtests once realistic execution/timing/selection/regime controls imposed.**
**(1) VRP:** VRP_t = E_t^Q[∫σ_s²ds] − E_t^P[∫σ_s²ds]; practical VRP_t = IVVar_t(T) − E_t[RV]; ex-post = IVVar − RV. "One of most robust findings in vol literature: index-option implied variance tends to exceed subsequently realized variance on average — compensation for vol/crash risk, not free arb. Documented across equity indices and multiple asset classes" [afajof.org]. **HONEST HORIZON NOTE: strongest evidence at weekly→monthly→multi-month horizons, NOT intraday.** Intraday "VRP" dominated by: bid/ask bounce, non-sync option/underlying prices, auction effects, IV interpolation, time-of-day seasonality, event jumps, risk-neutral-vs-noisy-realized mismatch. 5-min/1-day implied-vs-realized = market-state feature, NOT the established academic premium. Defensible test: implied vs future realized over matched 7/30/60/90-day horizons. Failure: earnings/CPI/FOMC/elections/jumps, vol-crash/panic, short-vol crowding, persistent vol trends, equity-risk-premium shifts, mismatched maturities, selling variance when tails underpriced. "Negative realized premium over a sample ≠ long-run premium gone."
**(2) Straddle-implied vs realized:** M_straddle ≈ (C+P)/S (or /F forward-consistent); one-std-style implied move only under simplifying assumptions, NOT guaranteed range. Earnings: M_realized = |S_after−S_before|/S_before; short-vol profitable pre-cost when M_realized < M_straddle (P&L depends on strike/skew/vega/hedging/execution/exit). Literature: IV elevated pre-announcement, falls after; realized move often below implied ON AVERAGE. NOT universal "fade every straddle": varies by firm, event, IV percentile, option liquidity, executable vs midpoint quotes. Report conditional hit rate = #{M_realized<M_implied}/#events. Published hit rates **mid-50%–low-60%** for broad liquid samples (varies by period/methodology). **"60% hit rate not automatically attractive: 5–10% option round trip can erase small avg edge"** [tradingview.com]. Honest backtest reports: raw hit rate, avg implied-minus-realized, avg P&L/straddle, median, worst decile, bid/ask execution, exclude overlapping macro events, by IV percentile + vol regime. Failure: genuine information shocks, guidance changes, crowded shorts, macro overlap, stale/illiquid surface → low implied move, too much tail convexity sold, delayed post-event exit.
**(3) Unusual options activity → stock direction:** large trades CAN contain info, but "unusual volume" ≠ bullish/bearish. Score U_{i,t} = volume_{i,t}/median(volume_hist); map via call/put, delta, trade direction, apparent opening. Academic: option volume, signed order flow, informative activity CAN predict subsequent stock returns — "statistically meaningful but generally modest, heterogeneous, strongest over short horizons" [scielo]. **CRUCIAL DATA PROBLEM = open/close flag.** Trade record: contract/price/size/timestamp/exchange/sometimes aggressor side — often NOT whether opened or closed. Large call buy may = open bullish / close bearish / spread leg / dealer hedge / closing after earlier purchase. OI changes help only after-the-fact, EOD granularity. Even w/ flags, multi-leg + dealer/customer classification hard. Signed delta flow ≈ Σ_j q_j Δ_j M_j = signed traded exposure, NOT confirmed customer opening. Failure: spread-dominated activity, index/ETF hedging, liquid/fast-diffusion names, near expiry, stale quotes/midpoint prints, signal formed after stock moved, vendor misclassifies aggressor side, hedge ≠ directional bet. "Treat as ranking/event-detection feature, not binary 'call buying = stock up' rule."
**(4) GEX/pinning:** GEX(K) = Γ(K)[OI_C−OI_P]MS²(0.01); GEX_total = Σ_TΣ_K GEX(K,T). Pinning mechanism: hedging flows dampen/reinforce near concentrated strikes; near-expiry large gamma concentration → increased price clustering around major strikes. Academic expiration/clustering work supports existence, but "magnitude not constant; depends on liquidity, moneyness, expiry proximity, regime." **Effect sizes modest — "cents or small fraction of a percent," NOT reliable intraday magnet.** Gamma walls "visually impressive, little OOS forecasting value." **Full-chain OI non-negotiable:** need OI by strike+expiry, C/P classification, multiplier, spot/forward, T, IV/gamma inputs, corp-action/adjustment data. NTM chain alone CANNOT support total GEX / zero-gamma / dominant-wall claims; missing far-wing/short-dated/index positions can flip aggregate sign. Even complete OI ≠ observed dealer positioning — call-minus-put sign convention is an ASSUMPTION; publish multiple sign conventions; distinguish "OI-based gamma exposure" from "dealer gamma." Failure: spot far from wall, macro event, gap across strikes, not close enough to expiry, stale/corrected OI, incomplete chain, OTC/structured hedges dominating listed, wrong dealer sign, trending vol.
**(5) Put/call contrarian:** PCR_vol = put vol/call vol; PCR_OI = put OI/call OI; delta-weighted PCR_|Δ| = ΣPutVol|Δ|/ΣCallVol|Δ| (often more informative). Contrarian hypothesis: high put demand = fear → rebound; low = complacency → weakness. "Empirical support for sentiment/option-demand measures predicting returns, but NONLINEAR + REGIME-DEPENDENT, not fixed threshold rule" [scielo]. Standardized: z_t = [PCR_t − median(PCR_{t−L:t−1})]/[1.4826·MAD]; condition on: high/low PCR, index vs single-stock, volume vs OI, delta-weighted vs counts, high/low VIX, trend regimes. Same high PCR can mean: panic near low / rational pre-crash hedge / persistent bearish regime / put overwriting or collars / index-fund protection. Failure: sustained bears, credit stress, vol breakouts, deleveraging, strong trends, institutional systematic hedging, ETF/index dominating ratio, expiry-week OI distortions. "Better as conditional sentiment-state variable than standalone buy/sell signal."
