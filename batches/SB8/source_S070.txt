**70. Straddle-implied move vs realized** [SR]
- Intuition: The ATM straddle prices the expected move; realized moves systematically under- or over-shoot it around events — fade realized moves beyond the implied move, or buy realized when implied is rich.
- Formula (standard desk reconstruction): ImpliedMove = (C_ATM + P_ATM)/S for the expiry; compare with subsequent realized |return| or high-frequency RV; signal = (realized − implied), z-scored; fade extremes. Into the event/expiry.
- Data: Options quotes + underlying price.
- Sources: OPRA/options vendors, equity feeds.
- Horizon: into the event/expiry.
- Citations: Practitioner standard; trading rule flagged [SR].
- Coverage: R1.
