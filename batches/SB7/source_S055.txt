**55. Johansen VECM cointegration** [D]
- Intuition: Multivariate generalization of Engle–Granger: find all cointegrating vectors among N legs jointly.
- Formula: VECM ΔX_t = ΠX_{t−1} + Σ_{i=1..k} Γ_i·ΔX_{t−i} + ε_t; rank(Π) = cointegrating rank via trace/max-eigenvalue tests; spreads = β′X_t for each cointegrating vector β.
- Data: Synchronized prices, N legs.
- Sources: Same as entries 49–50.
- Horizon: spread half-lives.
- Citations: Johansen (1988, 1991).
- Coverage: G.
