**53. Pair-quality filter — zero crossings (Do–Faff)** [D]
- Intuition: Among candidate pairs, those whose spread crossed zero most often in formation have the most reliable mean reversion.
- Formula: Count zero-crossings of the formation spread; rank pairs by crossings; trade only top-ranked pairs with the entry-49/50 rules.
