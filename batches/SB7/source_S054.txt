**54. Copula pairs trading** [D]
- Intuition: Linear correlation misses tail dependence; the copula of the joint return distribution flags mispricings that conditional probabilities catch.
- Formula: Fit marginals + copula C(u,v) on pair returns (e.g., t-copula) in formation; signal = conditional probability P(U_A < u | U_B = v) deviating from 0.5 (or a mispricing index = conditional − unconditional); enter/exit on conditional-quantile extremes.
- Data: Return series, daily or intraday.
- Sources: Any OHLCV vendor.
- Horizon: hours–days.
- Citations: Academic copula-pairs literature (Liew & Wu; Stander, Marais & Botha).
- Coverage: R1, G.
