**90. Variance-ratio / Hurst regime toggle** [D]
- Intuition: Detect whether the tape is in a trending (momentum) or choppy (reversal) regime and switch the signal family accordingly.
- Formula: VR(q) = Var(r^{(q)}_t)/(q·Var(r_t)); VR > 1 → trending (use momentum), VR < 1 → mean-reverting (use reversal). Hurst H from R/S analysis: H > 0.5 trending, H < 0.5 reverting.
- Data: 1-min returns, rolling windows.
- Sources: Any intraday feed.
- Horizon: regime label valid minutes to hours.
- Citations: Lo & MacKinlay (1988) variance ratio; Hurst (1951).
- Coverage: R1, C.
