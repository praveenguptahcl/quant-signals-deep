**58. Futures calendar spread (term-structure carry)** [D]
- Intuition: The term structure of futures is anchored by carry; calendar-spread deviations from fair value mean-revert.
- Formula: Fair F(T) = S·e^{(r−q−y)T}; calendar signal = (F_2 − F_1) − (fair_2 − fair_1); trade toward fair net of financing/storability. **Distinguish** from entry 60 (predictive lead-lag, not fair-value).
- Data: Futures quotes across expiries + rates/dividends.
- Sources: Databento CME, CQG, dxFeed.
- Horizon: minutes–days.
- Citations: Cost-of-carry model.
- Coverage: G, R1 (3.7 adjacent).
