**47. Bid–ask bounce exploitation** [D]
- Intuition: Transaction prices oscillate bid↔ask even when the mid is flat; a print at the ask is likelier followed by one at the bid.
- Formula: Roll DGP: Δp_t = s/2·(Q_t − Q_{t−1}) + u_t. Public rule: fade a print at the ask toward mid (and vice versa). Live MM bounce capture (queue position, cancels) is proprietary.
- Data: Trades + NBBO.
- Sources: TAQ, Databento MBP-1. Without quotes this is just the Roll estimator (entry 12), not a trade.
- Horizon: next 1–20 trades (ms–seconds); after costs an HFT/MM feature, not paper-trading alpha.
- Citations: Roll (1984); Stoll; Ho–Stoll inventory models.
- Coverage: G, R1 (as microstructure effect).
