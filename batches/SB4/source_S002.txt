**2. Multi-level / integrated OFI (MLOFI)** [D]
- Intuition: Deeper book levels carry additional, partially independent price-impact information beyond the touch.
- Formula: Size-based OFI computed per level over the first *L* levels (level-wise event-flow bid minus ask), summed or combined into one regressor (integrated OFI, Cont–Cucuringu–Zhang).
- Data: L2 depth event stream, ≥5–10 levels.
- Sources: Databento MBP-10, LOBSTER, exchange ITCH/PITCH.
- Horizon: seconds to minutes.
- Citations: Xu, Gould & Howison (2018/2019), arXiv:1907.06230.
- Coverage: R1, G, M.
