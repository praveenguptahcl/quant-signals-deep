**5. Multi-level book pressure (static depth imbalance)** [SR]
- Intuition: Aggregate bid vs ask depth across the first *L* levels captures latent supply/demand beyond the touch.
- Formula: BP = (Σ_{l=1..L} Q^b_l − Σ_{l=1..L} Q^a_l)/(Σ_{l=1..L} Q^b_l + Σ_{l=1..L} Q^a_l).
- Data: L2 snapshot, 5–10 levels, seconds frequency.
- Sources: Databento MBP-10, exchange depth feeds, dxFeed.
- Horizon: seconds to minutes.
- Citations: Practitioner standard; academic grounding in multi-level OFI (entry 2).
- Coverage: R1, G, M.
