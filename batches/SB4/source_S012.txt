**12. Roll implied effective spread** [D]
- Intuition: Bid–ask bounce induces negative lag-1 covariance in transaction-price changes; that covariance identifies the spread from prices alone.
- Formula: Spread = 2√(−Cov(Δp_t, Δp_{t−1})); convention: floor at 0 (or use |·|, Harris 1990) when covariance ≥ 0.
- Data: Transaction prices only — no quotes needed.
- Sources: Any trade tape, Polygon.
- Horizon: estimated over minutes to a day; liquidity/cost feature, not a directional trigger.
- Citations: Roll (1984).
- Coverage: R1, G, M, C.
