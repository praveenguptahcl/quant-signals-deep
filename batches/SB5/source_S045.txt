**45. Stretched-move z-score & market-internals extremes** [SR]
- Intuition: A move of k realized-vol units without matching volume/news is a liquidity excursion; market-wide tick/advance-decline extremes mark intraday capitulation.
- Formula (reconstruction): z = (P − VWAP_session)/(σ_realized·√t); fade when |z| > 2–3 and volume is not expanding; cover at z → 0 or a time stop. Market internals: TICK = (#NYSE uptick stocks − #downtick); TRIN = (Advances/Declines)/(AdvVol/DecVol); fade extreme TICK readings (±800–1000, desk-specific) or TRIN spikes.
- Data: Mid/last + session VWAP + realized vol; internals from NYSE TICK/TRIN feeds.
- Sources: Any intraday feed; dxFeed, Databento, broker feeds for internals.
- Horizon: 10 min–few hours (fades fast).
- Citations: Standard z-score mean reversion; Arms index (TRIN). Exact internals levels are desk-specific.
- Coverage: G.
