**33. Open-auction imbalance continuation** [SR]
- Intuition: A large opening-auction imbalance leaves residual pressure that continues for the first minutes of continuous trading.
- Formula (standard reconstruction): signed opening imbalance (or open print vs pre-open indicative price); trade with the sign for the first 5–15 minutes.
- Data: Opening-auction imbalance feed / opening print vs reference.
- Sources: Exchange feeds, Databento Imbalance.
- Horizon: 5–15 minutes.
- Citations: Practitioner standard.
- Coverage: R1, T.
