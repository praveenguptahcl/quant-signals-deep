**30. Bollinger bandwidth squeeze → expansion** [SR]
- Intuition: Compression of the bandwidth precedes volatility expansion; the subsequent band break has directional follow-through.
- Formula: Bandwidth = (upper − lower)/mid; when bandwidth sits at a low percentile of its recent history (squeeze), enter on a close outside the band in the break direction. (TTM Squeeze adds a Keltner confirmation — vendor overlay.)
- Data: OHLC, 1–15 min or daily.
- Sources: Any OHLCV vendor.
- Horizon: hours on 15-min bars; days on daily.
- Citations: Bollinger; squeeze rule is a standard practitioner reconstruction.
- Coverage: G, T, C.
