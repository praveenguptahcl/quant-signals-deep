**16. Hasbrouck information share (venue price discovery)** [D]
- Intuition: When the same instrument trades on multiple venues, decompose which venue's innovations drive the common efficient price — a cross-venue lead-lag feature.
- Formula: Fit a VECM on cointegrated venue mids; from long-run impact vector ψ and innovation covariance Ω = FF′: venue-j share = ([ψF]_j)²/(ψΩψ′); bounds via Cholesky order permutation. Complement: Gonzalo–Granger component share.
- Data: Synchronized mids/trades across venues, ≤1 s.
- Sources: TAQ multi-exchange quotes, Databento multi-venue.
- Horizon: estimated on minutes–days; used as a venue-routing/lead-lag feature.
- Citations: Hasbrouck (1995).
- Coverage: G, M.
