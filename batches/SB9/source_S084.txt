**84. Hasbrouck (1991) trade–quote VAR** [D]
- Intuition: Joint dynamics of quote revisions and signed trades decompose price moves into trade-correlated (information) vs trade-uncorrelated components.
- Formula: VAR in (r_t, x_t) (log-quote revision, signed trade); impulse response of r to an x shock = information content of a trade; variance decomposition = informativeness share.
- Data: Tick trades + quotes, synchronized.
- Sources: TAQ, LOBSTER.
- Horizon: seconds–minutes.
- Citations: Hasbrouck (1991).
- Coverage: G.
