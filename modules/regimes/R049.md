### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

VIX term-structure state: the spread between 3-month constant-maturity VIX futures and spot VIX. Backwardation (futures below spot) = the market pays for immediacy = fear now; steep contango = complacency = short-vol weather. The 3-month point is constant-maturity interpolated — never the raw front contract across a roll.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Spot `V_t`, 3-month constant-maturity futures `F^{(3m)}_t`:

$$S_t = F^{(3m)}_t - V_t$$

`BACKWARDATION = 1[S_t ≤ −2]` [example]; `CONTANGO = 1[S_t ≥ +2]` [example].
Worked checks [example]: `(18, 22)` → `S = +4` → CONTANGO; `(32, 28)` →
`S = −4` → BACKWARDATION; `(20, 20.5)` → `S = +0.5` → FLAT.

*Formula tag: [example] — constants inside this formula inherit it per the tag law
(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*

**Data inputs.** VIX index; 3-month constant-maturity VIX futures.

**Cadence.** Daily.

**Threshold bands.**

|---|---|---|
| `≥ +2` | CONTANGO — short-vol weather | [example] |
| `−2…+2` | FLAT | [example] |
| `≤ −2` | BACKWARDATION — fear now | [example] |
