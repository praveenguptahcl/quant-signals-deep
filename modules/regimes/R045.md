### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Cross-sectional volatility regime: the sample (ddof = 1) standard deviation of per-name 20-day [example] realized volatilities across a fixed universe. Wide = vol-of-vol is high = dispersion-trading weather; narrow = vol is uniform = short-vol carry weather. (Grok's worked example stated `1.320`% [unverified] but the supplied return set implies a recomputed sample σ of `1.2903`% [measured] — neither figure is used; this module's panel is synthetic [example].)

## §R3 Exact calculation

Per-name 20-day [example] realized vol `σ_{i,t}` (decimal, same units):

$$XS_t = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N-1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(\sigma_{i,t} - \bar{\sigma}_t)^2}$$

Hand-verified panels (8 names, vols in % [example]). Panel 0:
`[1.2, 0.9, 1.5, 1.1, 1.8, 0.7, 1.4, 1.0]` → `XS ≈ 0.3546`% → NORMAL.
Panel 1: `[2.5, 1.0, 3.0, 1.2, 2.8, 0.8, 2.2, 1.5]` → `XS ≈ 0.8565`% → WIDE.

(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*
