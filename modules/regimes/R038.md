### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Liquidity state as realized-vs-baseline: cumulative session volume divided by the 20-day [example] same-time baseline, and quoted spread divided by its baseline. Thin = low volume ratio and/or wide spread ratio. The baseline window is fixed ex ante — changing it flips labels on trending volume.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Cumulative volume `V_t` vs baseline `B_t` (20-day [example] same-time
mean):

$$\mathrm{liq\_ratio}_t = \frac{V_t}{B_t}, \qquad
\mathrm{spr\_ratio}_t = \frac{s_t}{\bar{s}}$$

Thin flag: `THIN_t = 1[liq_ratio_t < 0.70]` [example] or spread > `2`×
[example]. Worked check: `V = 0.55·B` → ratio `0.55 < 0.70` → THIN;
`V = 0.95·B` → NORMAL.

*Formula tag: [example] — constants inside this formula inherit it per the tag law
(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*

**Data inputs.** Intraday volume bars; NBBO spread; 20-day same-time baselines.

**Cadence.** Intraday (5-min bars).

**Threshold bands.**

| `liq_ratio` | State | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| `< 0.50` | SEVERE — stand down | [example] |
| `0.50–0.70` | THIN | [example] |
| `≥ 0.70` | NORMAL | [example] |
