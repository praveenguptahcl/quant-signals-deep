### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Name-level (or calendar-season) state: calendar distance to the next scheduled earnings release is small AND implied vol is elevated versus the name's own history (IV rank/percentile over 252 sessions [example]). IV crush after the print is the release mechanism. Timestamp accuracy is load-bearing: after-hours vs before-open matters, and close-to-close event returns do not demonstrate intraday tradability.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Calendar distance `d = T_e − t` (calendar days, signed). IV rank over a
252-session [example] lookback:

$$\mathrm{IVR}_t = 100 \times \frac{\#\{s \in \text{trailing 252} : IV_s \le IV_t\}}{252}$$

Proximity flag: `EARN_t = 1[d ≤ D] · 1[IVP_t ≥ IVP_crit]` with `D = 2` [example],
`IVP_crit = 90` [example]. Worked check: reports in `2` days, ATM IV at the
90th [example] percentile → `d = 2 ≤ 2` and `IVP = 90 ≥ 90` → PRE-EARNINGS.

*Formula tag: [example] — constants inside this formula inherit it per the tag law
(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*

**Data inputs.** Company earnings calendar (with report session); ATM IV (or IVX) time series.

**Cadence.** Daily; intraday on report days.

**Threshold bands.**

| `d` / IVP | State | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| `d ≤ 2`, IVP `≥ 90` | pre-earnings high-IV | [example] |
| `d ≤ 5` | watch | [example] |
| else | normal | [example] |
