### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

High-yield option-adjusted spread level and momentum — the market's summary statistic for default fear and risk appetite. Stress is a level *and* speed call: `OAS_t` plus its 20-session change. State is slow-moving; the Gate rejects chapters that day-trade off it.

## §R3 Exact calculation

$$\Delta_{20}\mathrm{OAS}_t = \mathrm{OAS}_t - \mathrm{OAS}_{t-20} \quad [\mathrm{bps}], \qquad
\mathrm{speed}_t = \Delta_{20}\mathrm{OAS}_t / 20 \quad [\mathrm{bp/day}]$$

Optional robust z on the change (second estimator):
$$z^{\mathrm{OAS}}_t = \frac{\Delta_{20}\mathrm{OAS}_t - \mathrm{med}(\Delta_{20}\mathrm{OAS})}
{\mathrm{MAD}(\Delta_{20}\mathrm{OAS}) \cdot 1.4826	ext{ [documented]}$$
Worked check: `OAS_t = 620` bps [example], `Δ_20 = +110` bps [example] → level
above `600` (stress zone), speed `+5.5` bp/day — fast widening → ON.
