### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Liquidity commonality: the mean pairwise Pearson correlation of daily changes in Amihud illiquidity (|r| / dollar volume) across a fixed universe. High commonality = liquidity is a common factor = crowded exits all fail together. Zero-volume bars are excluded (Amihud undefined) — never interpolated.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Amihud illiquidity `ILLIQ_{i,d} = |r_{i,d}| / DVOL_{i,d}`, daily change
`ΔILLIQ`. Mean pairwise correlation over window `W = 63` days [example]:

$$\bar{\rho}^{liq}_t = \frac{2}{N(N-1)} \sum_{i<j} \rho(\Delta ILLIQ_i, \Delta ILLIQ_j)$$

Hand-verified panel (6 names × 5 days, Amihud changes in % [example],
co-moving): mean pairwise `ρ ≈ 0.9931` → COMMON. A fragmented panel would read
near `0` or negative → FRAGMENTED.

*Formula tag: [example] — constants inside this formula inherit it per the tag law
(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*

**Data inputs.** Daily Amihud illiquidity per name; fixed universe; window W.

**Cadence.** Daily.
