## §R2 Precise definition

Variance decomposition of close-to-close returns into overnight and intraday components: r_cc = r_ON + r_ID with r_ON = open/prev-close − 1 and r_ID = close/open − 1. The overnight share s = σ²_ON / σ²_cc (with σ²_cc = σ²_ON + σ²_ID + 2·Cov). Sample variances (ddof = 1) throughout. Use total-return-adjusted series — corporate actions corrupt the close/open alignment silently.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Log or simple returns, sample (ddof = 1) variances over the window:

$$\sigma^2_{cc} = \sigma^2_{ON} + \sigma^2_{ID} + 2\,\mathrm{Cov}(r_{ON}, r_{ID}), \qquad
s_t = \frac{\sigma^2_{ON,t}}{\sigma^2_{cc,t}}$$

Hand-verified panel (5 days [example]):
r_ON = [0.004, 0.001, 0.005, −0.002, 0.003], r_ID = [0.003, 0.002, 0.004, 0.001, 0.002] [example]
→ `σ²_ON = 7.7e-6`, `σ²_ID = 1.3e-6`, `Cov = 2.9e-6`, `σ²_cc = 1.48e-5`
→ `s ≈ 0.5203` → BALANCED. (Grok's stated `0.626` for its own example could not
be reproduced from the data supplied with it — independently recomputed `0.7072`;
neither figure is used here. This module's panel is synthetic [example].)
