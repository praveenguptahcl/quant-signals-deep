### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Joint tail-risk state: co-occurrence of extreme moves across assets, measured by the fraction of benchmarks jointly in their tails (co-exceedance). Crisis if ≥ 3 of 8 benchmarks breach on the same day [example], or 2 consecutive days with ≥ 2 [example]. Crisis state requires Gate-level human acknowledgment to *trade into* — the fail-safe default action on crisis is reduce, never add.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Per benchmark `i`, return `r_{i,t}`, rolling mean `μ_{i,t}` and std `σ_{i,t}`
over lookback `L`:

$$z_{i,t} = \frac{r_{i,t} - \mu_{i,t}}{\sigma_{i,t}}, \qquad
C_t(\kappa) = \sum_{i=1}^{M} \mathbf{1}[z_{i,t} < -\kappa]$$

Crisis flag: `CRISIS_t = 1[C_t(κ) ≥ K_crit]` with `κ = 2.5` [example],
`K_crit = 3`, `M = 8` [example]. Worked check: z-values
`−3.1, −2.8, −2.6, −1.2, 0.5, −0.8, 1.1, −2.0` → `C_t = 3 ≥ 3` → CRISIS.

*Formula tag: [example] — constants inside this formula inherit it per the tag law
(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*

**Data inputs.** Daily returns on M=8 benchmarks (equity regions, credit, FX, commodities); lookback L; threshold κ.

**Cadence.** Daily.

**Threshold bands.**

| `C_t` | State | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| `0–1` | normal | [example] |
| `2` | watch | [example] |
| `≥ 3` | crisis — stand down directional; only liquidity-provision-at-extreme-spread or flat | [example] |
