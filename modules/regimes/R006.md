### R0.5 Lag contract (mandatory)

- **Indicator causal rule:** the indicator at time `t` is computed using only

  bar `t` finalizes.
- **Gating rule:** a regime label computed at `t` may gate a signal evaluated
  at `t` **only for trades executable at `t+1` or later** — never for fills
  inside bar `t`.
- **Backtest lag:** any backtest conditioning on this regime must lag the
  label by ≥ `1` bar [default] (min_lag = `1` bar [default]; VVIX dated t is known after Cboe publish; gates next-session sizing).

  `label_ts > indicator_ts` for the earliest gated trade and that appending a
  synthetic breakout bar does not move the trailing percentile used at `t`.
- **unknown_behavior:** `UNKNOWN` is restrictive — downstream halves size /
  widens stops / stands down; never treated as benign (F1).

## §R1 Precise definition

Market-implied VoV: Cboe VVIX — VIX methodology applied to VIX options, i.e. expected 30-day vol of the 30-day forward VIX [documented]. Historical (realized) VoV: `VoV = stdev(ΔIV_30d, 20d) / mean(IV_30d)` (coefficient of variation of IV changes).

## §R2 Exact calculation

Index: VVIX level and its percentile vs `252`-day [default] history [documented definition].

$$VoV = \frac{\mathrm{stdev}(\Delta IV_{30d},\ 20d)}{\mathrm{mean}(IV_{30d})} \quad\text{(single-name construction)}$$

VVIX descriptive ranges from market commentary (illustrative, not Cboe official cuts): `< 90` low, `90–150` normal, `> 150` stress.

**Data inputs.** VVIX close (index points); single-name 30-day ATM IV series for the historical leg.

**Cadence.** daily.

**Threshold bands.**

| Condition | Label | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| `VVIX < 85` | calm | [example] |
| `85 ≤ VVIX ≤ 110` | normal | [example] |
| `VVIX > 110` | stressed (vol itself is unstable) | [example] |

## §R3 Efficacy modulation

| Affected family | Direction | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Vol-targeting / risk-parity overlays | **inverts** | dies when VoV is high — the vol estimate you size on is itself noise; leverage whipsaws. Sizing error ∝ VoV |
| Vega-weighted strategies | **degrades** | edge exists but mark-to-market variance explodes; reduce |
| Directional signals | **neutral** | second-order effect; use as a sizing input, not a trigger |

## §R4 Loop spec (Sentinel / Verifier / Grok-Researcher / Adversary / Gate)

- **Sentinel:** computes VVIX percentile and single-name VoV; publishes RSV.
- **Verifier:** cross-checks the IV-change series against a second surface vendor; requires state agreement (calm/normal/stressed) or `UNKNOWN`.
- **Grok-Researcher:** proposes bands; leads only.
- **Adversary:** injects a VIX-option microstructure spike with no SPX jump; checks the label does not leak into equity sizing.
- **Gate:** forces `UNKNOWN` (never a sized label) when any input is `UNKNOWN` under high VoV — do not size on noisy vol.
- **F1–F5 instantiated:** F1 missing/stale → `UNKNOWN` (restrictive downstream);
  F2 bounds check on every emission; F3 dual-estimator agreement within the
  stated tolerance or `UNKNOWN`; F4 staleness TTL `3`× cadence → `UNKNOWN`;
  F5 no ex-post backtest-period selection without a pre-registered definition.

## §R5 Cost interface: regime state → cost-function adjustment

High VoV inflates vega-book P&L variance: the adjustment shrinks vega sizing and widens required edge for vol-of-vol sellers.

```python
def cost_adjustment(rsv, base):
    adj = dict(base)
    adj['trade_ok'] = True
    adj['size_mult'] = 1.0  # [default]
    if rsv['state'] == 'stressed':
        adj['size_mult'] = 0.5     # [example] halve vega sizing
        adj['edge_mult'] = 1.5     # [example]
    return adj
```
