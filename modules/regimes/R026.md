### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Whether inflation prints are surprising up or down: the standardized surprise `z = (actual − consensus) / σ_surprises` on CPI/PPI/PCE releases, plus the 5y5y breakeven 20-day change as a slow-moving confirm. The regime exists only in a tight window around the print — before it the state is pre-release compression, after it repricing/digestion — and the label flips only *after* the `08:30` ET print, never before.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Per release `r`, with actual `y_r` and pre-release consensus `C_r` in the same
convention (YoY or MoM percent):

$$s_r = y_r - C_r, \qquad z^{\pi}_r = \frac{s_r}{\sigma^{\pi}_L}$$

$\sigma^{\pi}_L$ is the sample standard deviation of `s` over the trailing
`L = 12` releases [example]. Confirm (slow): `ΔBE_20d = BE_t − BE_{t−20}` on the
5y5y breakeven. Worked check: actual `3.5`%, consensus `3.1`%,
`σ = 0.2`% [example] → `s = 0.4` pp, `z = 2.0` — a large positive surprise
(hawkish). The surprise construction is the standard z-score (textbook).

*Formula tag: [example] — constants inside this formula inherit it per the tag law
(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*

**Data inputs.** BLS CPI-U (or core) release actual; pre-release consensus median frozen at `08:29` ET [example freeze time]; trailing `12`-release surprise σ [example]; 5y5y breakeven daily series.

**Cadence.** Event-driven on release days (CPI `08:30` ET [documented release time]); breakeven monitor daily.

**Threshold bands.**

## §R4 Efficacy modulation

Direction ∈ `amplifies | degrades | inverts`; mechanism states *why*.

| Affected S/T families | Direction | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| T: rates/FX intraday reaction strategies | amplifies | genuine news → informed repricing; surprise days are the highest-efficacy sessions of the month |
| S/T: pre-release positioning fades | degrades | spreads widen `3–5`× into the print [example]; the fade's edge is adverse selection |
| T: duration overlays | inverts | a hot surprise inverts long-duration bias — condition down, never through |

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Sentinel | ingests the economic calendar + the frozen consensus feed and computes `z` |

| Adversary | tests pre-print leakage: consensus timestamp must be strictly before release timestamp; injects a post-print revised consensus |
| Gate | rejects any backtest that conditions on the print without the `08:30` ET timestamp guard |
