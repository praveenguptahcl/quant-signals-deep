### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Ownership concentration: HHI over holder weights w_i (fraction of shares outstanding held), plus the effective number of holders N_eff = 1/HHI. The tail (holders outside the top ten) is modeled as equal-weighted over `tail_names = 490` [example] holders — a stated assumption, not a measurement. 13F is quarterly with a `45`-day lag [documented-as-identifier]; derivatives and shorts are invisible.

## §R3 Exact calculation

Holder weights `w_i`, top-10 disclosed, tail equal-weighted:

$$HHI = \sum_{i=1}^{10} w_i^2 + N_{tail}\left(\frac{1-\sum_{i=1}^{10}w_i}{N_{tail}}\right)^2, \qquad
N_{eff} = \frac{1}{HHI}$$

Hand-verified panels (synthetic [example]). Snapshot A top-10:
`[0.072, 0.065, 0.058, 0.051, 0.044, 0.038, 0.033, 0.029, 0.025, 0.022]`,
tail `490` equal holders → `HHI ≈ 0.0224399`, `N_eff ≈ 44.56` → CONCENTRATED.
Snapshot B: `[0.06, 0.055, 0.05, 0.045, 0.04, 0.036, 0.032, 0.028, 0.024, 0.02]`
→ `HHI ≈ 0.0175894`, `N_eff ≈ 56.85` → MODERATE.
