### Lag contract

- The indicator is causal: the value/state published for time `t` is computed
  using only inputs with timestamps `≤ t` (Appendix e v1.0.0).
- A regime label finalized at `t` may gate signals only for trades at `t+1`
  or later — `earliest fill @open(t+1)` per the bar-label rule.
- Backtests: regime-conditioned variants lag the label by `≥ min_lag_bars` =
  `1` bar(s) [default]; no ex-post period selection (F5).
- No-lookahead check: for every prefix of the input series, the label at `t`
  is identical with and without later data (enforced in `test_no_lookahead`).

## §R2 Precise definition

Trading-day distance to the next scheduled FOMC/ECB/BOJ decision: pre-event drift/compression vs event-day repricing vs post-event digestion. Documented facts: equities drift up `+49` bp in the 24 hours before scheduled FOMC vs `~4` bp on a normal day (Lucca–Moench (2015) [documented]); volume falls before and rises after (Zhu/BIS lineage, per Grok [unverified]); the 30-minute statement window (T−10m to T+20m) is the high-frequency identification convention (Gürkaynak–Sack–Swanson (2005) [documented]).

## §R3 Exact calculation

Let `T*` be the announcement timestamp, `t` now:

$$\tau = \frac{T^* - t}{\text{1 trading day}} \qquad (\tau > 0 \text{ is pre-event})$$

Window map (operational):
`τ > 25` far · `2 < τ ≤ 25` drift window [example bins] ·
`0.5 < τ ≤ 2` pre-announcement day · `−0.02 < τ ≤ 0.5` event/30-min shock window ·
`τ ≤ −0.02` post-digest. The 24h pre-window is close-to-5-minutes-before-release
(Lucca–Moench [documented]). High-frequency policy shock: Kuttner /
Bernanke–Kuttner / GSS surprises from FF futures around the statement.

(Appendix i v1.0.0); worked-check numbers carry their own tags.*

**Data inputs.** FOMC calendar (unscheduled meetings handled separately); ECB/BOJ calendars; FF futures for the shock (phase 2).

**Cadence.** Daily; intraday on event days.

## §R4 Efficacy modulation

Direction ∈ `amplifies | degrades | inverts`; mechanism states *why*.

| Affected S/T families | Direction | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| T: breakout strategies | degrades | pre-event directionless drift kills breakouts — false breakouts into the event |
| T: intraday reaction strategies | amplifies | event window is the highest intraday efficacy *after* the statement — genuine information, one-sided flow |
| T: post-event momentum | amplifies | institutional repositioning takes days — follow-through or reversal day |
| T: short-gamma / event-blind mean-reversion | degrades | reduce initiation into the event; vol expansion is the base case |
